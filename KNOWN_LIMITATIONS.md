# Prototype boundaries

- Fictional local demonstration only. No real patient information, clinical decision support, emergency dispatch, hospital partnerships, appointment guarantees, SMS, payments, uploads, bed/queue feeds, or real referral delivery.
- Four personas and scoped mock-repository guards demonstrate behavior. They are not authentication, confidentiality, or tamper resistance. A trusted backend is required for production permissions and immutable server audit.
- Browser localStorage is best-effort. Private browsing, quota, profile deletion, and device changes can lose demo state. Same-origin tabs synchronize and detect intervening stored updates, but simultaneous writes are not atomic; use one presenter for the main journey.
- All facility names, contacts and coordinates are synthetic. Approximate Isabela City center provides context only. Do not navigate to these markers seeking real care.
- Directions use public OSRM driving routes over OpenStreetMap roads for a low-volume, non-commercial demo. Availability and map coverage are best effort. Estimated driving time is not traffic-aware, and this is not turn-by-turn navigation. Dedicated authorized routing is needed for sustained use. A failed lookup shows no route line; retry and Google Maps remain available. Fictional off-road pins snap to mapped roads within 1 km, with offsets displayed instead of drawing off-road connectors. Discovery list distances remain explicitly labeled straight-line distances.
- Road requests send starting/destination coordinates to the public routing service, which may log them. No patient or referral information is sent. Device coordinates remain transient in the app and are not saved in localStorage.
- OSM tiles need connectivity and are best effort. The list and workflow remain usable when blocked. A different tile operator may be needed at scale; no offline tiles or bulk downloads are offered.
- Location is optional, permission-dependent and transient. Low-accuracy positions over 1km are rejected with manual recovery guidance. Background/phone-lock tracking is not guaranteed. Location is not shared with staff.
- 60-minute report freshness is a presentation assumption, not a clinical standard. Fixtures are refreshed on Reset Demo. Scheduled hours and reported availability do not guarantee a doctor or acceptance.
- PWA install support varies. The service worker supplies an offline document and icons only; it does not reopen the entire interactive application offline after a cold start. There is no background synchronization.
- Browser/native select and date popups intentionally follow platform behavior; one English locale and light theme are implemented. Automated Chromium/axe checks do not establish WCAG certification, full screen-reader usability, real-device touchscreen behavior, Safari/iOS install reliability, or clinical usability.
- A referral draft is prepared through the form and only persisted when submitted; abandoning unsaved work prompts. There is no separate draft autosave. The seeded Ana draft demonstrates review/submission.
- The sharing acknowledgement is a demo review step, not proof of informed consent, policy compliance, or regulatory readiness. No public clinical QR or record URL is generated.
- Operation errors surface safe messages with local developer-console operation names. No remote telemetry, secure developer dashboard, retention service, or alerting is configured.
